(03) — About

Hi — I'm Noa.

Motion designer, ten years in. Barcelona.

I started in a broadcast basement cutting promos. Now I animate for the brands that used to buy those slots.

I work solo or embedded with studios, usually from boards through to final grade. Most of my week is Cinema 4D and After Effects, with Houdini when something needs to break, burn or pour.

I like briefs with a real constraint in them — a six-second cap, a fixed palette, a logo that has to survive being thrown across a stadium screen. Away from the desk I shoot 16mm and lose at chess.
