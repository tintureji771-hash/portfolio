(01) — Ethos

We are a small studio by choice. Fewer clients, deeper work, and the stubborn belief that taste still compounds.

Unfold began as two people and a shared frustration: strategy decks that never became anything. So we made a studio where thinking and making live in the same room, on the same week, under the same name.

Today we're a team of eleven working across brand systems, digital products and moving image — for founders on their first raise and institutions on their hundredth year.

(04) — How we work

Four moves,
no theatre

Typically eight to fourteen weeks, with something real in your hands by week three.

STEP 01

Listen

Interviews, audits, and an honest read of where the brand actually stands — not where the deck says it does.

STEP 02

Frame

One page of strategy. Position, audience, tone, and the single idea everything else has to earn its place against.

STEP 03

Make

Two directions, taken far enough to judge. We iterate in the open, weekly, with you in the file.

STEP 04

Land

Production files, systems, and a handover session so your team can run it without calling us. They usually do anyway.
