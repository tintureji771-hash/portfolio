About Me

Who is Noa Vega?

I started in a broadcast basement cutting promos. Ten years later I animate for the brands that used to buy those slots — solo, or embedded with studios, usually from boards through to final grade.

Most of my week is Cinema 4D and After Effects, with Houdini when something needs to break, burn or pour. I like briefs with a real constraint in them: a six-second cap, a fixed palette, a logo that has to survive a stadium screen.

My Process

The Way I Work

01

Brief & Research

What it's for, who it's for, and where it plays. Reference boards before anything moves.

02

Boards & Animatic

Frames and a timed animatic with scratch sound, so we agree on pacing before it gets expensive.

03

Animate & Render

Blocking, then polish, then lookdev. Weekly WIPs — you see it move long before it's finished.

04

Deliver

Every ratio and cutdown you need, plus project files and a handover call.
